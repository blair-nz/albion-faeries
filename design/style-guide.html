A stylish, fun Albion Fae look

Light-forward, a little wild, warmly queer: dew on fern, foxglove in the hedge, gathering laughter after rain. Built for a simple public hearth - not another black sidebar dashboard.

Direction

Name: Foxglove Glade. Mood: fabulous without costume-shop kitsch; pagan without gothic gloom; British countryside without cream-and-terracotta cafe branding. The brand name is the hero. Imagery of real places and faces does the rest.

Hero atmosphere sample Albion Faeries An online space of fabulousness for the Radical Faeries of old Albion.

Design principles

  • Brand first - "Albion Faeries" is the loudest mark on the first screen.
  • One composition - home is a hearth, not a three-column control panel.
  • Real glade - photos of gatherings, halls, land; not stock sparkles.
  • Kind clarity - accessible type, plain words, mobile-first.
  • Fun with restraint - foxglove accents and soft motion, not emoji confetti.

Colour

Cool mist grounds keep us away from warm-cream cliches. Fern carries stewardship. Foxglove is the queer pulse. Pollen is rare - for highlights, not fills.

Lean into

  • Mist-to-dew atmospheres and photographic overlays
  • One foxglove action per view
  • Deep fern for type hierarchy

Steer away from

  • Black left-rail + white dashboard grids
  • Purple-to-indigo default "queer web" gradients
  • Pill forests, neon glow, emoji bullets

Typography

Fraunces for brand and headlines - soft optical serif with a wink. Figtree for body and UI - friendly, readable, not Inter/Roboto.

Voice

Warm, clear, a little camp. Never corporate. Never gatekeepy. Invite first; explain second.

An online space of fabulousness for the Radical Faeries of old Albion.

Keep the inherited line - it already sounds like us.

Say

  • "Come along to a circle or gathering."
  • "What's on this moon."
  • "Gatherings Circle can publish the call."

Avoid

  • "Synergise your spiritual journey."
  • "Members-only portal unlock."
  • Jargon that needs a wizard.

Components

Few pieces, used with intention. Cards are for things people act on (an event, a form) - not for wrapping every paragraph.

Imagery

Full-bleed heroes from Albion life: Featherstone halls, Glastonbury land, circle tables, muddy boots, glitter if it actually happened. Prefer one dominant photograph over collages. No floating sticker labels on the hero.

Yes

  • Edge-to-edge gathering photos
  • Natural light, imperfect joy
  • Consent-aware faces and credits

No

  • Inset rounded media cards in the hero
  • Generic stock "mystical forest"
  • Word-cloud identity graphics as the main idea

Accessibility and care

Aligned with Digital Homecoming: accessible, kind, simple before clever.

  • Body text at least 17px; long lines capped near 70 characters.
  • Foxglove on mist checked for contrast on buttons (white label).
  • prefers-reduced-motion disables drift and long entrances.
  • Focus rings in pollen/fern - never remove outlines.
  • Public vs Circle-only content stays clearly separated.
  • Minimum personal data on forms.
  • Plain-language alternatives beside faerie poetry.
