Direction

Name: Hot Spore. Mood: festival heat meeting forest floor - dual-tone grain, heart-capped mushrooms, a shy smile in the dark. Brand name still leads; the textures do the atmosphere.

Hero on dune texture Albion Faeries An online space of fabulousness for the Radical Faeries of old Albion.

Design principles

  • Brand first - "Albion Faeries" still owns the first screen.
  • Texture as place - dunes and wood are the landscape, not wallpaper sprinkle.
  • Doodles as charm - mushrooms, spiral, smile mark sections and empty corners.
  • Hot, not harsh - cream reading surfaces between loud bands.
  • Hand-made edge - slightly wonky energy; avoid corporate polish.
  • Two thresholds - public heat; Circles go quieter on void + gold.

Heat textures

Original grain fields made for this kit - same mood as the examples Yellow shared (hot dual-tone, stipple, organic flow), not those project files. Use one dominant texture per band. Keep type large and high-contrast on top.

Lean into

  • Hero and gathering banners on dunes or wood
  • Dark scrim under type for readability
  • Alternating texture bands down long pages

Steer away from

  • Both textures fighting in the same viewport
  • Tiny repeating wallpaper behind dense body copy
  • Purple-indigo glow stacks on top of the grain

Illustrations

Yellow's doodles sit on our heat textures as cream-ink cutouts - soft, hand-drawn, a little shy. Use as section marks and companions, not as a second logo fighting the wordmark.
