Option 2 · Deep Glade

Deep England, living green

A sun-dappled woodland hearth: moss canopy, leaf-light, a stream under the trees. The forest moves - a looping film as the atmosphere, not a static stock still.

Web-optimised loop from the project art file (~0.6 MB H.264, muted, poster still for reduced motion). Full-bleed behind the page; a soft canopy veil keeps type readable.
