Get involved

We are an informal network of connected individuals. Come to a circle or gathering, join the mailing list, or find us on Faenet and Facebook.

Come along

Start with Gatherings & Events. No application form - arrive with care. If you are new to Faerie or queer space, you can write first and ask.

Other ways to connect

Faenet is a private Faerie social space. The Facebook group Radical Faerie UK has regional links and gathering chat. Slack is used by co-organising Circles — request an invite via email.
