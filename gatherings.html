What is a gathering?

If you have never been, you may wonder what happens. Activities change from one gathering to the next. What tends to return is a fluid, diverse queer space where people of many ages, races, bodies, sexualities, genders, and beliefs come together — liberating for some, unfamiliar for others. If you are new, you can chat with an organiser first. If your heart is already saying yes, listen to that.

Co-creation

Faeries say: if you see something that needs doing, the best way is to do it. A team helps beforehand; the event itself happens because of who comes. People chip in — meals, dishes, wood, workshops, heart circles, entertainment, sweeping up. Nothing particular is obligatory; participation is the gathering.

Food, heart, and play

We eat together — vegetarian with vegan options, cooked in the kitchen by whoever wants to help. Heart circles often run freer of city time-pressure and can open profound sharing. Workshops and activities may include ritual, dancing, drumming, theatre, music, art, games, fancy dress, silence, solitude, chaos, and more. If you bring it, it will most likely happen.

Care and conduct

A gathering aims to be inclusive, safe, and supporting. Organisers may speak with people when behaviour causes conflict, and may ask someone to leave to protect the whole. It is not group counselling — you hold responsibility for your own personal, emotional, physical, and sexual safety. Heart circles, understanding faeries, hugs, and organisers are around with support and love.

How we pay

Bank details are shared after booking; cash at the gathering is fine too. We hold NOTAFLOF close — No One Turned Away For Lack Of Funds. Gatherings are non-profit; additional donations are gratefully received.

Northumberland

Featherstone Castle

Samhain · Ostara · autumn & spring gatherings

  • Log fires
  • Vegetarian feasts
  • Country walks
  • Heart circles
  • Dancing and drumming
  • A place to play

A collaborative, co-created gathering — cook, chop wood, hold workshops, keep the castle cared for. The drag room offers a new outfit for every adventure.

Vegetarian and vegan meals. The castle can be cold — bring warm layers, slippers, outdoor clothes, boots, and a torch. Bring your own bedding and pillowcase; beds and mattresses are plentiful.

Baths and showers with hot water — bring towels. Fancy dress welcome in the drag room. Auctions often help fund the gathering.

Ground floor is wheelchair accessible; dining and most social spaces are upstairs by stairs.

Featherstone Castle, Hall Bank, Northumberland NE49 0JG — outside Haltwhistle, near Hadrian’s Wall.

  • Train via Carlisle or Newcastle to Haltwhistle — book early.
  • By car: three miles south-west of Haltwhistle on the A69.
  • By air: Newcastle, then train to Haltwhistle.

Pre-book taxis — Haydonbridge +44 (0)1434 671197, Sproul +44 (0)7712 321064, Diamond +44 (0)7957 641222.

Arrive from noon on the first day; leave by noon on the last.

Somerset

Paddington Farm · Glastonbury

Imbolc · Beltane · summer & spring gatherings

A 43-acre organic farm at the foot of Glastonbury Tor. Gatherings are co-created — a core team holds the call and food; once we arrive, every contribution is welcome.

Heart circles, shared vegan/vegetarian meals in the long-house kitchen, and daily tasks. If you see something that needs doing, why not be the one to do it?

Rooms across several buildings; some uneven paths. Camping in warmer weather. Tell us access needs in advance. House beds usually include duvet, pillow, and bedding.

Bring fancy dress for the drag room if you like, auction gifts, wellies, slippers, wet-weather gear, and note dietary needs on the booking form.

Arrive after 4pm on the first day; leave by 11am on departure day.

Paddington Farm Trust, Maidencroft Lane, Glastonbury BA6 8JN — about 30 minutes’ walk from town.

  • Train: London Paddington to Bristol or Taunton, then bus onward.
  • Bus: Berrys to Glastonbury; National Express / Megabus to Bristol then local bus.
  • Taxi: pre-book Trotta’s 07879 333020 or JJ’s 07977 904064.
  • By car: approach from the west via Brindham Lane (///steady.rezoning.spurted).

East Sussex

Laughton Lodge

Lughnasadh · summer gathering

A residential community on the edge of the South Downs near Lewes. Albion Faeries gathered here for Lughnasadh among meadows, woodland, and shared outdoor space.

Shawfield holds a sprung-floor hall, kitchen, dining room, music room, guest rooms, and a patio with a fire pit.

Rural community setting with indoor rooms and land to roam. Bring layers for cool evenings and outdoor footwear. Exact bedding notes live with each gathering’s call.

Laughton Lodge, Common Lane, Lewes BN8 6BY — about 90 minutes from London. Stations: Lewes and Uckfield. Free parking on site.

Venue notes at laughtonlodge.org

London & local

What is a heart circle?

A Radical Faerie tradition for open, honest, loving communication. People meet in sacred space to speak from the heart and be listened to from the heart — with confidentiality, “I” statements, no debate or interruption, and a talisman that holds each person's turn. No one has to speak. It is not formal therapy, yet witnessing and being witnessed can be deeply healing.

Heart circles often sit beside drumming, workshops, and full-moon socials. Ask locally when the next one gathers — or open one yourself with care.
